**# Data Note – School Nutrition Programme Monitoring**



\## Purpose of the Dataset

This dataset has been created to demonstrate a \*\*district-level monitoring and reporting workflow\*\* for school nutrition programmes. It is designed to reflect how routine programme data is typically collected, reviewed, and used by district and block teams.



The dataset supports:

\- Coverage monitoring

\- Reporting compliance checks

\- Identification of data gaps

\- Follow-up planning



It is not intended for impact evaluation or outcome analysis.



---



\## Nature of the Data

\- \*\*Type:\*\* Simulated, programme-realistic data

\- \*\*Level:\*\* School-level

\- \*\*Time period:\*\* One reporting month

\- \*\*Geographic scope:\*\* One district with multiple blocks



The dataset is simulated to allow demonstration of monitoring logic in the absence of publicly available school-level programme data.



---



\## Unit of Observation

\*\*One row = One school × One reporting month\*\*



This mirrors real district trackers used for monthly programme reviews.



---



\## Key Variables Included

\- Identification: District, Block, School ID, School Name, School Type

\- Exposure: Enrolment, days school functioned

\- Programme delivery: Days meals planned and served

\- Supplementary inputs: IFA distribution, deworming

\- Reporting: Monthly report submission status

\- Remarks: Contextual notes from the field



---



\## Variables Deliberately Excluded

The following are intentionally not included:

\- Student-level nutrition status

\- Anthropometric measurements

\- Anaemia prevalence

\- Cost or financial data

\- Learning or academic outcomes



These are not part of routine monthly school nutrition monitoring and would reduce realism.



---



\## Data Limitations

\- Simulated data does not represent actual programme performance

\- One-month snapshot does not capture trends

\- No outcome or impact measures included



These limitations are appropriate given the monitoring-focused objective.



---



\## Ethical Statement

The dataset contains no personal or sensitive information. It is used solely to demonstrate monitoring, data management, and reporting workflows.
