## **# District School Nutrition Programme Monitoring Brief**



\## District: Sitapur  

\## Reporting Period: August 2024  

\## Programme Focus: School Nutrition \& Supplementary Interventions



---



\## Background

School-based nutrition interventions are essential for improving dietary intake and preventing anaemia among school-age children and adolescents. At the district level, effective implementation depends on regular monitoring of meal delivery, reporting compliance, and follow-up mechanisms.



District and block teams rely on routine school-level data to identify gaps and take corrective action.



---



\## Objective

The objective of this monitoring exercise was to:

> \*\*Demonstrate a district-level school nutrition programme monitoring system using routine-style data, focusing on coverage, reporting compliance, and follow-up readiness.\*\*



This exercise does not assess programme impact.



---



\## Data \& Scope

\- Simulated, programme-realistic dataset

\- One district with three blocks

\- Thirty schools

\- One reporting month



The dataset is designed to mirror real reporting formats used in district programmes.



---



\## Methodology

\- School-level data organised in a raw reporting format

\- Validation checks applied using Excel formulas

\- Derived indicators created for monitoring

\- Monitoring tables prepared at school, block, and district levels



Raw data values were not altered during validation.



---



\## Monitoring Outputs



\### School-Level Tracker

Provides a detailed view of programme delivery and reporting status at each school, enabling targeted supervision.



\### Block-Level Summary

Aggregates school data to identify blocks with higher reporting gaps or lower coverage.



\### District Summary

Presents a consolidated snapshot for district leadership and reporting to state teams.



\### Follow-up Action List

Identifies schools requiring immediate follow-up due to reporting or coverage gaps.



---



\## Programme Use

The outputs can be used for:

\- Monthly district review meetings

\- Planning supervisory visits

\- Strengthening reporting compliance

\- Supporting convergence discussions with education and health teams



---



\## Ethical \& Data Use Statement

This monitoring exercise uses simulated programme data and does not involve individual-level information. Outputs are descriptive and intended solely for programme monitoring and support.



---



\## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how routine school-level data can be structured and used to support district-level monitoring of school nutrition programmes. The approach is practical, replicable, and aligned with real programme management workflows.
